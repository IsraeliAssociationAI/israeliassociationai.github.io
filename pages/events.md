## Future Events
* [ISFAI 2027](https://iaai.org.il/events/ISFAI2027), the annual Israeli Symposium on the Foundations of AI, will be held on February 10th, 2027, at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa.



## Past Events
* [AI Session @ National Computer Science Conference 2026](https://conferences.bgu.ac.il/cs2026/artificial), including the annual IAAI meeting, was held on October 8th, 2026, at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Beer-Sheva.

* [BISFAI 2025](https://sites.google.com/view/bisfai25), the biennial Bar-Ilan Symposium on the Foundations of AI, was held on September 9th and 10th, 2025, at Bar-Ilan University.

* [IAAI 2024](https://iaai24.net.technion.ac.il/), the annual IAAI conference, was held on September 24th, 2024, at the College of Management Academic Studies, Rishon LeTsiyon.

* [BISFAI 2023](https://sites.google.com/view/bisfai-2023), the biennial Bar-Ilan Symposium on the Foundations of AI, was held on February 1st and 2nd, 2023, at Bar-Ilan University.

* [IAAI 2022](https://iaai22.net.technion.ac.il), the anual IAAI conference, was held on June 16th, 2022, at the IBM Research Labs, Haifa.
